Details

The athlete who earns the most points from the club's allotted races becomes this season's club champion, one score per gender age group, best four races count.

How it works

1

The committee picks 16-22 races for the season.

2

Win your gender age group and you score 100 points.

3

Don't win it? You get a bit less than 100, depending on how far behind the winner you were.

4

Your best 4 races count, and Club Champs is worth double. Max possible score: 500.

The Maths

Your gender age group winner's time ÷ Your time × 100 = Your points
Example: you finish in 50 minutes. Your gender age group winner does 47. 47 ÷ 50 × 100 = 94 points.

No place, no distance, no discipline — just your time against the winner's time in your age group. That's why a sprint tri and a full Ironman are worth the same, and why a 63-year-old woman and a 25-year-old bloke can both compete for their respective female and male Club Champion trophy. Points are calculated down to the last decimal place if needed, so there's no rounding of times or points involved.

Races and conditions

  • ✓

    In order to earn points you must be a financial member at the time of each race.

  • ✓

    To be eligible for the end of year awards you must have completed a minimum of two designated races and participated or volunteered in at least one club race (Triathlon or Duathlon, excluding the Richie Walker Aquathlon) during the season.

  • ✓

    To obtain Race Points at Club Champs in February 2027 you must be racing for Cronulla Triathlon Club.

  • ✓

    Club Champs is a Double Point Score Race.
