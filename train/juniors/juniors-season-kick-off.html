Welcome

We are excited to kick off the new triathlon season with a full day of clinics, goal setting and fun at Cronulla RSL, one of our major sponsors this year. Whether you're a first-time participant or a returning athlete, this day is all about getting to know your coaches and teammates, picking up some new skills and setting yourself up for a great season ahead!

A Note On The Clinics

You don't need to be super fit to take part, we cater for all levels, fromcomplete beginners to experienced Juniors. Much of the focus is on learning the fundamentals, but just as important is making sure everyone has funand gets to meet other Juniors starting out in this healthy, fun sport.
